# Qatar and Oman: Primary-source register for country pages

Date checked: 2026-11-24
Purpose: prepare accurate, country-specific pages without inferring medicine availability or turning city pages into pharmacy directories.

## Qatar

### Official legal source
- Qatar Legal Portal (Al Meezan), Law No. 2 of 1983 on the Practice of Human Medicine and Dentistry, Article 17: https://www.almeezan.qa/LawArticles.aspx?LawArticleID=9090&LawId=253
- The portal marks the law as in force. Article 17 sets the legal conditions for abortion and requires a government hospital and a three-specialist medical committee in the specified cases. Any page must quote/paraphrase the current official text accurately and be reviewed by a Qatar-qualified legal/medical reviewer before publication.

### Official medicine-registration source
- Hukoomi Qatar service, Apply for Registration of Pharmaceutical or Biological Product or Vaccine, provided by the Ministry of Public Health: https://hukoomi.gov.qa/en/categories/environment-health-and-safety/apply-for-registration-of-pharmaceutical-or-biological-product-or-vaccine
- This service describes pharmaceutical product registration and marketing authorization. It does not establish whether a specific product is currently registered, available in a particular pharmacy, or dispensed without prescription.

## Oman and Muscat

### Official legal sources
- Sultanate of Oman, Royal Decree No. 43/2025 issuing the Public Health Law, Article 15: https://qanoon.om/p/2025/rd2025043/
- The official text states that acts intended to cause abortion are prohibited except in cases specified by the law regulating them. Do not treat Article 15 alone as a complete statement of exceptions.
- Oman Medical Practice Law, Royal Decree No. 75/2019, Article 36: https://qanoon.om/p/2019/l2019075/
- Oman Penal Law, Royal Decree No. 7/2018, Articles 315-320: https://qanoon.om/p/2018/rd2018007/
- These provisions must be read together and checked for amendments and implementing decisions by a qualified Oman legal reviewer. Do not present an isolated article as complete legal advice.

### Official medicine regulator
- Oman Ministry of Health, Drug Safety Center: https://www.moh.gov.om/en/hospitals-directorates/directorates-and-centers-at-hq/drug-safety-center/
- The Drug Safety Center describes responsibilities including medicine regulation, product registration, pharmacy and pharmaceutical institution licensing, and post-marketing surveillance. Its Muscat location/contact information is published on the official page.

## Page architecture recommendation
- Create /cytotec-qatar only after legal review, with a distinct Qatar-specific explanation and direct links to Al Meezan and MOPH/Hukoomi.
- Create /cytotec-oman and /cytotec-muscat only if each page has distinct value; Muscat should add genuinely local official service information, not merely replace the city name.
- Do not state that Cytotec/Misoprostol is for sale, stocked, deliverable, or obtainable from a named pharmacy unless verified from a current authoritative source and lawful to publish.
- Separate product registration, prescription/dispensing requirements, legal exceptions, and clinical safety. They are different questions.
- Add visible source publication/review dates and a named qualified reviewer. Never fabricate reviewer credentials.
- Until review is complete, keep the new routes out of the sitemap and do not publish them.
